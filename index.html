About NautIQ Solutions ⚓

NautIQ Solutions provides independent maritime inspections and audits designed to give shipowners and operators clear, practical insight into the true condition of their vessel operations.

Like seasoned navigators reading complex waters, we help shore management identify gaps between onboard practice, the vessel’s Safety Management System (SMS), and current industry standards. Our focus is not simply compliance, but clarity; ensuring management teams understand operational risks, performance weaknesses, and areas requiring attention before they escalate into incidents or deficiencies.

With extensive seagoing experience combined with shore-based operational expertise, we bring a balanced, practical perspective to every audit and inspection. Our work supports safer vessels, stronger systems, and more confident decision-making at both sea and shore.

As NautIQ Solutions continues to grow, we remain committed to delivering high-quality assurance services that strengthen operational discipline, enhance compliance, and keep vessels firmly on course in an increasingly demanding maritime environment.

Vision

To be a trusted maritime partner delivering specialist expertise and practical solutions that enhance safety, compliance, and performance across global shipping operations.

Mission

To deliver high-value maritime auditing and inspection services that identify operational gaps, strengthen vessel management, and ensure ships are consistently prepared for external inspections.

Values

Guided by integrity, clarity, and operational discipline, we act with professional courage, respect the vessel's crew, protect confidentiality, and identify risks before they escalate.

NautIQ Solutions is founded and led by Blessen Joe Mathews, a Master Mariner with more than two decades of hands-on experience across the maritime industry. His seagoing career began in 2001. In 2005, he joined a leading LPG shipowner and operator and, from 2006 onward, sailed and worked exclusively within its gas tanker fleet. He has worked as a Marine Superintendent for the world's leading owner and operator of Very Large Gas Carriers (VLGCs), specializing in the global transportation of liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) for five years. In May 2025, he began working independently as a freelance auditor, work he now carries out under NautIQ Solutions.

Blessen holds a Certificate of Competency for Master of Foreign Going Ship and a Diploma in Nautical Science, and is a DNV-certified ISM, ISPS, and MLC auditor for shipping companies. His career has taken him from the deck of LPG carriers through to the superintendent's office, managing fleets across Singapore, Oslo, and Australia.

As Marine Superintendent, Blessen managed six to eight vessels at a time on a day-to-day basis, overseeing dry-dock planning and execution, Ship-to-Ship operations, and closing out SIRE, CDI, PSC, and ISM audit observations with corrective and preventive actions. In the past six years, he has personally carried out more than 300 ship audits and inspections, spanning static and dynamic navigational audits, cargo and mooring audits aligned with OCIMF SIRE 2.0, and pre-purchase, pre-sale, and pre-hire inspections for owners, operators, and third-party clients.

That background underpins every audit NautIQ Solutions carries out. Having managed vessels from the shore side and closed out findings from class societies, oil majors, and vetting inspectors, we know exactly what an audit needs to deliver to be useful, not just as a compliance record, but as a clear, practical picture of where a vessel and its systems really stand.
